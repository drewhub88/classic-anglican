# Classic Anglican Theme

A WordPress theme designed to give a church site a classic, elegant look that works seamlessly on mobile, tablet, and desktop.

---

## 📖 Project Overview

This theme was built to create a web presence that feels uniquely sacred rather than like a standard corporate or generic modern landing web page. It prioritises classic, elegant aesthetics and easy navigation for everyone.

## 🎨 Design Rationale

### Colour Palette: Maroon, Navy, Gold & Off-White
The theme relies on maroon, navy, and gold—colours typical of many Anglican websites, but styled here to feel classic and parish-focused rather than corporate or overly modern.

### Classic Serif Typography
I paired Playfair Display and Lato for typography. Playfair Display gives headings and key passages a classic, traditional prayer-book aesthetic, while Lato handles long paragraphs to keep reading crisp on mobile devices.

### Dynamic Hero Section & Default Visual Anchor
The full-screen hero section features a slider built to be the first thing visitors notice when the page loads. By default, it features stained-glass photography—chosen because traditional architecture and light filtering through glass naturally evoke a sense of sacred space. However, the hero slider is completely dynamic and customizable. The site owner can easily replace or remove the default images entirely to showcase real-life photographs of their own parish building, church events, or community activities.

### Fully Responsive Design
The theme is engineered to be fully responsive. Whether parishioners are checking service times on a mobile phone, reading theological essays on a tablet, or browsing on a desktop computer, the layout adapts cleanly without compromising accessibility or visual appeal.

### Welcoming Newcomers
A prominent **"Plan Your Visit"** call-to-action is built directly into the first slider with another placed just above the footer section—hooking newcomers when they first open the site and giving them a clear next step after scrolling through.
